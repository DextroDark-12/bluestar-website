Our Story

Three generations of stone craftsmanship — rooted in tradition, built for modern spaces.

A Legacy of Stone

BlueStar Granite & Marble was founded in 2003 in Jalandhar, Punjab, by the third generation of a family of stonemasons. What began as a small workshop has grown into one of northern India's most trusted natural stone suppliers.

Our founders learned the trade from their fathers — men who shaped stone by hand for palaces, havelis, and temples across Rajasthan and Punjab. That same eye for quality lives on in every slab that leaves our yard.

Today, BlueStar sources granite, marble, quartzite, and sandstone from the finest quarries in India and abroad. Our mission remains simple: deliver premium natural stone with honest advice, fair pricing, and reliable delivery.

"We don't just sell stone. We carry forward a craft our grandfathers trusted us to preserve."

— Purav, Owner & Director

Our Journey

Key milestones that shaped BlueStar.

2003

Founded

BlueStar Granite & Marble opens its doors in Jalandhar, Punjab, focusing on premium Indian granite for local contractors and architects.

2008

Expanded to Marble

Added marble sourcing from Makrana and Italy, broadening the collection to serve luxury residential and hospitality projects.

2013

CNC Fabrication Unit

Invested in a state-of-the-art CNC fabrication unit, enabling precision cutting, edge profiling, and custom sink cutouts in-house.

2018

Pan-India Shipping

Launched secure crating and insured shipping to all 28 states and 8 union territories, making BlueStar stone accessible nationwide.

2024

500th Project Completed

Reached the milestone of 500 completed projects — from kitchen countertops to large-scale commercial cladding — across India and abroad.
